🎭 Separate fine-grained emotion model

GoEmotions is a 28-label multi-label task, separate from three-class sentiment.

FULL SOURCE SPLITS
The One-vs-Rest Logistic Regression baseline was trained on 43,066 deduplicated training texts with thresholds tuned on 5,408 validation texts. On the unchanged official test split of 5,427 texts, the validation-tuned thresholds yield micro-F1 0.5238 and macro-F1 0.4502; a fixed 0.5 threshold yields micro-F1 0.4853 and macro-F1 0.4267. Exact-text overlap between the deduplicated modeling splits is zero. This remains a modest baseline, particularly for rare labels.

Technical notes

About the model

What runs, what it outputs, and where it can fail.

Model used

This app uses face-api.js with its Tiny Face Detector and Face Expression Recognition neural network. The library provides seven expression classes: happy, sad, angry, fearful, disgusted, surprised, and neutral.

Model weights are loaded from the face-api.js project's public weights directory. The app reports a model-loading error if those files cannot be reached. No prediction is generated when the model is unavailable.

TensorFlow.jsTiny Face Detector7 expression labelsBrowser inference

Inference pipeline

  • Request camera permission and display the live video.
  • Run face detection on a throttled interval.
  • Only continue when exactly one face is detected.
  • Run the expression model and read its seven output scores.
  • Update charts and history using actual results.

Dataset and training

This app uses pretrained public model weights. The app does not train a model in your browser and does not independently verify the original training dataset or its evaluation metrics. FER-2013 is a commonly used facial-expression research dataset, but this app does not claim its deployed weights were trained or tested on FER-2013.

Limitations

  • Lighting, camera quality, face angle, and occlusion can affect outputs.
  • Model performance can vary across people and settings.
  • Facial expressions are ambiguous and context-dependent.
  • A probability score is not a calibrated measure of emotional certainty.
  • Do not use results for diagnosis, hiring, discipline, surveillance, or decisions about a person.
Your data

Privacy by design

You control camera access and can stop it whenever you want.

Camera permission

Your browser asks for permission before the camera starts. You can revoke permission in your browser settings. Camera access requires a secure context such as HTTPS or localhost.

Local processing

Video frames are sent to the in-browser face detector and expression model. This page does not upload camera frames to an application server, record video, or capture audio. Model library and weight files are fetched from a public CDN/repository when needed.

Session history

Prediction history and session statistics are kept in page memory while the page is open. They are not sent to a database. Use Export CSV if you choose to save your session, or Reset session to clear the in-page results.

Stop anytime

Select Stop Camera to stop the camera tracks and end analysis. Closing or refreshing the page also ends the camera session. The browser's camera indicator helps show when the camera is active.
